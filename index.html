Case · Farm operations, 2025

Nitrite kept spiking in the ponds.

High nitrite puts a whole crop at risk. The farm kept applying the usual fix, but it wasn't working.

  1. PROBLEMAll 4 ponds over the safe limit for weeks, despite the usual fix.
  2. WHY 1Why isn't the usual fix working?CheckedFollowed the agreed fix and tracked results daily.FoundIt kept coming back. The assumed cause didn't fit.
  3. WHY 2If it's low salinity, why didn't last year look like this?CheckedCompared like for like: same farm, same season, same salinity, one year apart.FoundA different pattern this year, so a different cause. Last year: rose together This year: one flat, nitrite spiking input that becomes nitrite nitrite · illustrative, not real values
  4. WHY 3What changed?CheckedMapped microbe dosing dates onto the daily water data.FoundNitrite jumped right after a new microbe product was added.
  5. WHY 4 · ROOT CAUSEWhy would one product do that?CheckedReviewed every product and dose with a lab expert and a vet.FoundIt did the same job as one already in use. Together, they threw the pond out of balance.
  6. WHY 5Why didn't the tests catch it?CheckedReviewed the bacteria results with the experts.FoundThe tests measure amount, not balance, so the counts looked normal.
  7. RESULTImproving in 2 days, normal in about 4. I ran the meeting to agree the fix, and the case was shared as a best practice with other regions.

Problem
A daily pricing tool worked in one region, but scaling it meant handling 50–60K rows of order data every day.
Action
Automated the data flow with Excel Power Query and rolled it out to 11 regions and 24 plants. Added plant-level fill-rate views to tell true supply shortages (raise price) from single-plant gaps (send to inter-plant transfer).

Starting point
Field work moves fast, so the natural focus is solving today's issues quickly. The opportunity was to add one more step: making sure the same issue doesn't come back, and linking daily work to the sales target.
Action
I coached them to find root causes and to plan from the target: break it into initiatives with numbers, and check they close the gap. I gave guidance and contacts, not answers, and let them do the work.
Result
A major pork customer threatened to switch after its main plant closed and deliveries from the backup plant ran late. I brought the trainees together with the logistics and plant heads to find the cause and agree a fix. The problem didn't come back, the customer stayed, and about ฿1M in sales was protected.

Harvest planning · 2025Team project · adoption on my farms

Turning an SOP formula into a tool farms used before every harvest

฿2–3M/year saved across 3 farms · later rolled out nationwide

Field data→SOP formulas→When & how much to harvest
Read the full storyClose→
Problem
Each crop is harvested in 3–4 partial rounds. Misjudge stock count or size and ponds get crowded, growth slows, sizes miss spec and prices drop, with knock-on effects for plants and sales forecasts. The SOP had the right formulas, but decisions were usually made from experience.
Action
My teammates had built the SOP formulas into an Excel template. The next step was getting farms to use it. Each of us took the farms we were responsible for. On mine, I introduced it to the farm managers, trained them, worked through the numbers with them every week, and made sure it was used before every harvest.
Result
About ฿2–3M saved a year across 3 farms, then rolled out to farms nationwide.

Problem
Quotes started from floor price, margins were thin, and key accounts were at risk over late deliveries.
Action
Segmented customers by preferred size and buying behavior. Quoted at list price, with end-of-day discounts only to clear stock. Re-sequenced routes with logistics and checked key-account orders daily.
Result
Sales from ~฿1.2M to ~฿3M a month, +฿0.2M/month margin with no volume lost, and ฿0.3M/month in key accounts retained.
